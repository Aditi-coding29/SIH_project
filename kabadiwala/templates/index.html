Business Logic & Integrity

The Real Circular Economy Model

Fair margins, dignified livelihoods, and corporate environmental responsibility

Phase 1: Segregation

Household User

Sells sorted waste at fixed fair rates. Receives immediate payout + CSR green cashback.

💡 Example: Sells 5 kg Plastic @ ₹10/kg = ₹50 paid in cash/UPI by Kabadiwala.
Core Economic Driver
Phase 2: Aggregation

Informal Kabadiwala

Buys scrap from users and aggregates in bulk. Sells to formal recyclers earning a profitable commercial margin.

📈 Margin Logic: Sells 5 kg Plastic to Recycler @ ₹13.50/kg = ₹67.50. Gross margin = ₹17.50 profit!
Phase 3: Circular Smelting

Formal Industrial Recycler

Purchases certified clean bulk scrap for smelting & pulping. Digitally confirms the recycling chain.

🌱 Closed Loop: Verified receipt triggers Green Reward Fund distribution to user & Kabadiwala.
Institutional Incentive Pool

Where does the User Green Cashback come from?

Cashback is funded entirely via corporate CSR sponsorships (Tata Trusts, Unilever, Godrej) & Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) recycling allocations. The Kabadiwala does NOT pay the cashback; they simply pay the scrap price!
